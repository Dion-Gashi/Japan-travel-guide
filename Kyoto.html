Kyoto

About Kyoto

Kyoto is famous for its traditional culture, historic temples, and beautiful shrines. It was the former capital of Japan and is known for its well-preserved history, peaceful atmosphere, and traditional Japanese architecture.

Popular Districts

Gion

Arashimya

Beautiful natural scenery with bamboo groves, rivers, and mountains. Perfect for walking and photography.

Kiyomizu

Area with historic temples, old streets, and souvenir shops. Ideal for culture lovers.

Top Attractions

  • Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)
  • Fushimi Inari Taisha
  • Kiyomizu-dera Temple

Things To Do

Explore temples, try matcha tea, walk through historic streets, experience traditional Japanese festivals.
