1st Tokyo

Tokyo is the capital city of Japan and one of the largest cities in the world. It is known for its modern skyline, advanced technology, shopping districts, and vibrant nightlife. At the same time, Tokyo offers traditional temples, local food culture, and unique neighborhoods to explore.

2nd Kyoto

Kyoto is famous for its traditional culture, historic temples, and beautiful shrines. It was the former capital of Japan and is known for its well-preserved history, peaceful atmosphere, and traditional Japanese architecture. Visitors often come to Kyoto to experience cultural heritage, nature, and classical Japanese cuisine.

Interested in Kyoto? Learn more here

3rd Osaka

Osaka is well known for its vibrant city life, friendly atmosphere, and amazing street food. It is often considered Japan’s food capital, offering popular dishes like takoyaki and okonomiyaki. The city combines modern entertainment, shopping areas, and historic landmarks, making it a lively destination for visitors.
