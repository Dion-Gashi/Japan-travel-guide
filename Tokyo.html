Tokyo

About Tokyo

Tokyo is the capital of Japan and one of the largest cities in the world. It combines modern technology with traditional culture.

Popular Districts

Shibuya

Famous for its busy crossing and nightlife. Perfect for shopping and young travelers.

Shinjuku

Known for skyscrapers, entertainment, and neon lights.

Asakusa

Traditional district known for its temples and historic atmosphere.

Top Attractions

  • Tokyo Tower
  • Senso-ji Temple
  • Shibuya Crossing

Things To Do

Try sushi, explore anime districts, visit temples, and enjoy the skyline at night
